Apply [OSDS Verification Gates](../osds-verification-gates/SKILL.md) as the shared safety contract, using the current user authorization.


Consult [architecture](../../../docs/architecture.md), current issuance/document services and the relevant B2/B3 acceptance report. These are synthetic demonstration documents, not official certificates.

- Keep application, request and document lifecycles separate: request draft/submitted is distinct from PDF processing/failed/completed. Approval or a saved draft does not establish generation eligibility.
- Verify current Director approval, exact immutable submission/request/definition bindings, protected identity values and evidence keys through the service contract. Do not collect new supplementary evidence or infer a stage from a broad application state.
- Use exact published template and matching module/routing-region personnel versions. Never borrow another region's personnel, withdraw preserved roots or alter captured routing to fit a fixture.
- Explicit generation and authorized downloads require fresh authority. Completion status alone does not verify stored bytes: check checksum, size, page count, linkage and required demonstration/nonofficial markings on every page when authorized.
- Preserve original PDFs and immutable attempt/history records. Missing/corrupt-file probes require explicit approval and ledger-owned auxiliary artifacts with verified backups; never damage a baseline original.
- After uncertain generation, inspect the saved outcome before any separately authorized action. Do not automatically replay generation or recovery, overwrite originals or fabricate failed/processing states.

Use source-only checks when runtime operations are closed. Report external evidence content/custody, official validity and human acceptance separately; do not infer them from reference syntax or a successful test render.

## Evidence and completion

Required evidence: current service contracts, immutable case/request/version linkage and authorized byte/page inspection results. Complete with per-artifact authorization, checksum/marking findings and explicit unavailable or human/external-content checks.
